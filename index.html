Interactive Gravitational Lensing Simulator

This interactive visualization demonstrates how massive cosmic objects bend the path of light from distant background sources. This warping of spacetime, predicted by Einstein's General Relativity, acts like a natural lens, magnifying, distorting, and sometimes creating multiple images or rings (like Einstein Rings) of the background objects.

The central bright spot represents the foreground 'lens' object (like a massive galaxy or black hole's gravitational influence), and the smaller points are background 'stars' or galaxies whose light is being bent on its way to you.

Instructions

Rotate: Click and drag your mouse across the screen.
Zoom: Use your mouse wheel.
Observe how the distortion changes with perspective and zoom level, especially for stars appearing close to the central object.
