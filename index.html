Interactive Gravitational Lensing Simulator

About this Simulation

This interactive visualization demonstrates how massive cosmic objects, such as black holes or galaxies, bend light from distant stars, creating stunning visual distortions in space.

Instructions

Click and drag your mouse across the screen to rotate the entire scene. This allows you to observe the gravitational lensing effect from different angles.

Use your mouse wheel to zoom in or out of the scene. Watch how the stars closer to the central massive object appear more distorted.
